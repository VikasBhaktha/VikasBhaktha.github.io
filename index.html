Hello, I'm Vikas.

I am a PhD Candidate at Wayne State University and a Bridge Engineer at Jacobs.

My work lies at the intersection of structural engineering, computational mechanics, finite element analysis, and scientific computing.

My research focuses on computational methods for structural analysis, uncertainty quantification, numerical simulation, and engineering applications involving complex structural systems.

I received my M.S. in Structural Engineering from the University of Southern California and expect to complete my PhD at Wayne State University in December 2026.

Research Interests

  • Computational Mechanics
  • Finite Element Analysis
  • Structural Engineering
  • Bridge Engineering
  • Uncertainty Quantification
  • Scientific Computing

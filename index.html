Structural Engineering · Computational Mechanics

Hello, I'm Vikas.

I am a PhD Candidate at Wayne State University and a Bridge Engineer at Jacobs, working at the intersection of computational mechanics, structural engineering, and engineering practice.

My research interests include finite element methods, structural simulation, uncertainty quantification, scientific computing, and computational approaches for understanding complex engineering systems.

In professional practice, I work on bridge engineering problems involving structural analysis, design, assessment, and transportation infrastructure.

I earned my Master of Science in Structural Engineering from the University of Southern California and expect to complete my PhD at Wayne State University in December 2026.

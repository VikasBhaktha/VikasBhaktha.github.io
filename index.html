Hello, I'm Vikas Bhaktha.

I am a PhD Candidate at Wayne State University (expected Dec'2026) and a Bridge Engineer at Jacobs.

My work lies at the intersection of bridge design, structural engineering & computational mechanics.

My research focuses on computational methods for structural analysis, structural reliability, numerical simulation, and engineering applications involving complex structural systems.

I received my M.S. in Structural Engineering from the University of Southern California in 2016 and B.E. in Civil Engineering from Sri Jayachamarajendra College of Engineering, India in 2015.

Research Interests

  • Structural Engineering
  • Computational Mechanics
  • Structural Reliability Methods

Hello, World!

My name is Vikas Bhaktha and I am a PhD Candidate at Wayne State University and a Bridge Engineer at Jacobs.

My work lies at the intersection of structural engineering, computational mechanics, finite element analysis, and scientific computing. I am interested in developing computational methods and engineering tools for solving complex structural and infrastructure problems.

I received my M.S. in Structural Engineering from the University of Southern California. I expect to complete my PhD at Wayne State University in December 2026.

In professional practice, I work on bridge engineering projects involving structural analysis, design, assessment, and transportation infrastructure.

Research Interests

  1. Computational Mechanics: finite element methods, numerical modeling, structural simulation, and computational mechanics.
  2. Structural Engineering: bridge engineering, structural analysis, infrastructure assessment, and structural systems.
  3. Scientific Computing: Abaqus automation, Python scripting, Fortran development, and engineering software tools.
  4. Uncertainty & Reliability: uncertainty quantification, probabilistic analysis, and structural reliability.
